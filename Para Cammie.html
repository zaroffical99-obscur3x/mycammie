Mi querida Cammie,

I could have sent you a message. Some things deserve more than that. They deserve a seal, a candle, and a letter that takes its time. So I built you one.

There is an ocean between us. There are two countries, two languages, and half a day of clocks. And still you are the first person I think of when I wake up and the last one I think of at night. Distance has never once made you feel far.

You love what is dark and tender at the same time. Red roses, black ink, stories that frighten you and break your heart in the same chapter. Maybe that is why we fit. I spend my days with things that live in the dark, and you are the one person who looks at them with me and is never afraid.

I still have not found the words for your eyes. I have stopped trying. I just look at them every time the screen lets me, and every time I forget what I was going to say.

You read people the way other people read books, and somehow you read me and stayed. Nobody has ever made me feel so understood and so unafraid of being understood.

Every day I choose you. On the good days, on the quiet days, and on the days when a small screen is the only window I have to you. I want to choose you on the day we finally stand in the same room, and on every ordinary day after it.

So here, in the only way I can from the other side of the world, I am asking the question I have never been surer about.

Te amo, mi Cammie. Para siempre.

Siempre tuyo,
